Gunung Bayu, north of the city, is climbed on foot from Basecamp Bayu: grab a water bottle and a torch at the gear point, follow the Pos 1 · Pos 2 · Pos 3 posts, refill at the blue AIR drums, watch your stamina and water, cross the broken bridge plank by plank, take your senter through the cave, dodge the rockfalls — and plant your flag at the puncak.
