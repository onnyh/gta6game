# BAYU HEAT

A 3D open-world crime game — part street crime, part mountain climb — that plays in a desktop browser from static files. No build step, no network at runtime, no downloaded assets: the city, the volcano, the traffic, the police and every sound are generated by code on top of a vendored three.js.

You are **Damar Wicaksono**, a driver working off a Rp 15.000.000 debt to **Ratna Sari** of Bengkel Ratna in **Teluk Bayu**, a fictional Indonesian coastal city: a working pelabuhan with cranes and perahu, a pasar tradisional, ruko rows, an alun-alun under the Tugu Bayu monument, a pantai boardwalk — and, inland, the volcano **Gunung Bayu** rising over the Pegunungan Jawa Tengah. Run Ratna's six jobs, steal anything with wheels, outrun the POLRES — or ditch the car at Basecamp and run the jalur pendakian, where no police car can follow.

## How to play

Serve the `game/` folder with any static file server (or run `node serve.ts` in this repository) and open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. Click the page once so the pointer locks and sound can start.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrow keys | Move on foot · drive |
| Mouse | Look / aim (pointer lock) · click to shoot or punch |
| F | Enter / exit any vehicle — cars, angkots, a Bebek, even cruisers |
| E | Talk · interact · advance dialogue |
| Space | Handbrake |
| 1 / 2 | Fists / pistol |
| R | Reload |
| N | Next radio track (while driving) |
| P or Esc | Pause — full map, stats, settings |
| M | Mute |

Follow the gold beacon. Crimes witnessed by pedestrians or the POLRES raise your **wanted stars** (1–5): cruisers route through the street grid and ram, officers bail out and chase on foot, roadblocks appear at four stars, and the GARUDA-1 helicopter's spotlight joins the hunt. Break line of sight and the heat cools — fastest in the mountains, where cars stop at Basecamp and foot officers give up part-way up the trail. Get caught and it's **BUSTED** (a fine at the POLRES); get put down and it's **WASTED** (a hospital bill at RS Bayu Medika).

## What's in the game

- **A 3D Indonesian coastal city** — golden-hour light and long shadows over a 7×7 street grid (Jl. Merdeka, Jl. Pantai Raya, Jl. Pelabuhan, Jl. Pasar Baru…), downtown office towers, ruko shophouse rows with WARUNG KOPI / BENGKEL / BAYUMART signs, a mosque silhouette, the Tugu Bayu monument, an SPBU-style BayuPetrol station, pasar stalls under striped awnings, a container port with portal cranes, and a beach with boardwalk, palms and animated sea.
- **Gunung Bayu and the climb** — a heightmap volcano inland with pine forest, rocks and a winding mountain road to **Basecamp** (parked angkots, a warung, the trailhead gate cars cannot pass). On foot: the marked jalur pendakian past Pos 1 / Pos 2 / Pos 3, slope-slowed movement, a **stamina** bar that drains uphill, cloud ring and cooler colours near the top, edelweiss flecks — and at the **puncak**, planting the Indonesian flag for a Summit Bonus. Backdrop cones Merbabu, Sumbing and Sindoro close the horizon. Pak Darto at the basecamp warung pays extra for a summit story.
- **Traffic and pedestrians** — cars, angkots and motorbikes drive the grid left-hand, brake, honk and reroute; sixteen pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and flee from gunfire and bad driving.
- **Steal anything** — Aleron GT coupe, Bruma sedan, Engkel van, Duna pickup, colorful angkots, a rideable Bebek motorcycle, and POLISI cruisers. Arcade driving physics with drift, damage and a condition meter.
- **A six-mission story** with staged objectives (STAGE n/n), dialogue briefings with portraits, timers, scripted chasers and star beats, cash rewards in Rupiah — including a blackout run that ends by ditching the car at Basecamp and escaping up the trail. The finale clears Damar's debt.
- **Combat and consequence** — fists and an unlockable pistol with magazines and reserve ammo, health + armour, officer gunfire, WASTED and BUSTED flows, REP levels from NEW FACE to LEGEND OF THE BAY, eight hidden cash stashes, health/armour pickups.
- **A HUD that mirrors the fantasy** — mission panel with stage bar and NEXT line, live POLRES response list (CAMAR-1, ELANG-2, RAJAWALI-3, GARUDA-1 with distances and states), circular minimap with police blips, money/stars/clock (weekday + HH:MM), weapon slots with ammo, speedometer with vehicle name, condition and the Tide 101.5 radio readout while driving.
- **Procedural WebAudio everything** — engine and bike notes, distance-based sirens, shots, impacts, jingles, and a four-track procedural radio station. No audio files.
- **Title screen, pause with full city + mountain map and stats, and a localStorage save** — money, REP, story progress, summit, stashes, stats and position persist.

## Layout

- `main.ts` — the hand-in program: copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to the output directory and writes `dossier.json` with the vendored library.
- `serve.ts` — a small local preview server for `game/`.
- `vendor/makefun-game.ts` — make.fun's dossier library, unchanged.
- `game/index.html` — the page and the whole HUD. `game/vendor/` holds three.js 0.187.0 (minified module build, vendored — never loaded from a network). `game/src/` holds the game, one job per file:
  - `config.js` tuning, street names, vehicle and wanted tables · `rng.js` seeded randomness · `state.js` the shared game state · `input.js` keyboard, mouse, pointer lock
  - `city.js` the city grid, districts, terrain heightfield and collision · `world.js` the 3D world: sky, buildings, port, beach, volcano, trail
  - `vehicles.js` kitbashed vehicles and arcade physics · `actors.js` low-poly people · `traffic.js` traffic AI and pedestrians
  - `police.js` wanted system, pursuit, roadblocks, trail officers, GARUDA-1 · `combat.js` hitscan, punches, damage
  - `missions.js` the six-mission story runner · `hud.js` HUD, dialogue, minimap, pause map and stats · `audio.js` procedural sound and radio
  - `save.js` the localStorage save · `shots.js` the staged scenes behind the screenshots · `main.js` boot, player control, cameras and the loop that wires it all together
- `screenshots/` — four real renders of the game running (staged with `shots.js`, captured in a browser, never edited or composited).
